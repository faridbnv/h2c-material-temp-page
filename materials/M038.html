PC-GF is glass-fibre-filled polycarbonate. Makers sell it for technical applications that need stiffness and heat resistance. Its glass transition is 142 °C, and makers warn that polycarbonate is highly hygroscopic, prone to stress cracking and notch sensitive. On the H2C it prints at 290–320 °C, and makers recommend a hardened steel nozzle against the abrasive glass and drying before printing.

Among the materials

Against PC, PC-GF is 55 % weaker across the layers and 37 % weaker flat. Against PC-ABS, PC-GF is 22 % weaker across the layers and 9 % weaker flat. Among the materials, PC-GF has a low tensile strength (Z) and price: both are in the lowest quarter of the materials that publish them. The rest of its key properties sit in the middle half.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), PC-GF typically reaches a tensile strength of 38 MPa at a modulus of 2.67 GPa, and breaks at 5.95 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 62.7 MPa at a flexural modulus of 2.4 GPa.

What makers say about printing it

Nozzle wear

  • Printing with Ultrafuse® PC GF30 may reduce brass nozzles and extruder driving wheels’ lifetime. BASF Forward AM Ultrafuse PC GF30, data sheet, p. 1
  • For a better experience, using hardened steel nozzles and extruder driving wheels is advised. BASF Forward AM Ultrafuse PC GF30, data sheet, p. 1
  • Hardened nozzle recommended. SIDDAMENT V2 PC-GF, data sheet, p. 1
  • When melted, Ultrafuse® PC GF30 filament can be abrasive due to its glass reinforcement. BASF Forward AM Ultrafuse PC GF30, data sheet, p. 1

Moisture sensitivity

  • PC is highly hygroscopic — store sealed with desiccant. SIDDAMENT V2 PC-GF, data sheet, p. 2
  • Keep filament sealed in its original packaging or a dry box until use SIDDAMENT V2 PC-GF, data sheet, p. 2
More from makers: printing advice and warping and shrinkage

Printing advice

  • Allow first-layer adhesion to set before increasing print speed SIDDAMENT V2 PC-GF, data sheet, p. 2

Warping and shrinkage

  • Reduced warping and improved strength under load. SIDDAMENT V2 PC-GF, data sheet, p. 1

Uses, limitations and chemicals

What it is sold for

  • Combines PC's high-temperature performance with the rigidity and dimensional stability of glass fibre. SIDDAMENT V2 PC-GF, data sheet, p. 1
  • The extreme stiffness makes this material highly suitable for demanding applications. BASF Forward AM Ultrafuse PC GF30, data sheet, p. 1
  • In addition to the high print quality, the high mechanical strength and the heat resistance, the glass fiber reinforced PC offers excellent impact strength and fracture toughness and is therefore the ideal choice for technical applications. Fabru Polycarbonate glass fibers 10% (PC GF10), data sheet, p. 1

What makers warn about

  • Caution is required with regard to stress cracks, the effects of hot water and acids / alkalis such as acetone. Fabru Polycarbonate glass fibers 10% (PC GF10), data sheet, p. 1
  • The component can then become very brittle and break or shatter without warning. Fabru Polycarbonate glass fibers 10% (PC GF10), data sheet, p. 1
  • Glass reinforcement may shorten brass nozzle and drive-wheel life BASF Forward AM Ultrafuse PC GF30, data sheet
