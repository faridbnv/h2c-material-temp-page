Nylon-CF, maker-undisclosed polyamide is a carbon-fibre-filled polyamide, a nylon. A maker sells it for jigs and fixtures that need increased toughness, and its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 145 °C. A maker notes that its mechanical properties may change with water uptake. On the H2C makers call for a hardened or ruby nozzle, because carbon fibre is abrasive, and for feeding it dry.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), Nylon-CF, maker-undisclosed polyamide typically reaches a tensile strength of 41 MPa at a modulus of 2.7 GPa, and breaks at 18 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 90.7 MPa at a flexural modulus of 3.59 GPa.
