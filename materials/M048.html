PAHT-CF is a carbon-fibre-filled high-temperature polyamide. A maker suggests it for functional prototypes, machining fixtures, injection moulds and jigs. Semicrystalline, it carries light load well above its glass transition, and its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 145–194 °C. Across the layers it keeps 31 % of its flat tensile strength, and on the H2C it needs a hardened nozzle and drying.

Partly documented Price rests on 1 product, notched Charpy impact and HDT at 0.45 MPa on 2 products each and tensile modulus (XY), tensile strength (XY) and tensile strength (Z) on 3 products each. Among the materials

Against PA6-CF, PAHT-CF is 92 % stiffer, 47 % stronger flat and 21 % weaker across the layers. Against PA6-GF, PAHT-CF is 93 % stiffer, 60 % tougher in notched impact and 38 % weaker across the layers. Among the materials, PAHT-CF has a high tensile modulus (XY), tensile strength (XY), tensile strength (Z), notched Charpy impact and HDT at 0.45 MPa, and a low elongation at break (XY): each is in the top or bottom quarter of the materials that publish it. Its density and price sit in the middle half.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), PAHT-CF typically reaches a tensile strength of 103 MPa at a modulus of 8.39 GPa, and breaks at 1.98 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 161 MPa at a flexural modulus of 8.26 GPa.

Thermal properties

Its products put HDT at 145⁠–⁠194 °C under 0.45 MPa and 92⁠–⁠170 °C under 1.8 MPa, against a glass transition of 70 °C. Semicrystalline, it carries light load well above its glass transition. As published, Vicat softening is typically 216 °C and melting typically 230 °C.

After printing

Two products publish values measured after annealing, on schedules from 80 °C for 12 h to 90 °C for 2 h.

  • The annealing process can lead to a minor deviation in the dimensional accuracy. BASF Forward AM Ultrafuse PAHT CF15, data sheet, p. 4
  • the suggested annealing temperature of models printed with Bambu PAHT-CF is 80 to 130 °C, and the time is 6 to 12 hours. Bambu Lab PAHT-CF, data sheet, p. 3

What makers say about printing it

Moisture sensitivity

  • Ultrafuse® PAHT CF15 filament should be stored at 15 - 25°C in its originally sealed package in a clean and dry environment. BASF Forward AM Ultrafuse PAHT CF15, data sheet, p. 1

Nozzle wear

  • Nozzle Diameter ≥ 0,6 mm, hardened BASF Forward AM Ultrafuse PAHT CF15, data sheet, p. 3
More from makers: supports and removal, printing advice, warping and shrinkage, adhesion between layers and odour and emissions

Supports and removal

  • When printing a model with support, we recommend removing the support structures within 2 hours after printing to prevent them from sticking after absorbing moisture. If sticking occurs, dry the model to ease the removal. Bambu Lab PAHT-CF, maker's product page or guide, p. 1

Printing advice

  • Fix larger parts to avoid potential deformation during the annealing process. BASF Forward AM Ultrafuse PAHT CF15, data sheet, p. 4

Warping and shrinkage

  • No warping. LEHVOSS LUVOCOM 3F PA CF 9742 BK, data sheet, p. 1

Adhesion between layers

  • It is easy to print, has enhanced Z-bonding, a high-degree of dimensional stability, and has been optimized to retain excellent mechanical properties when wet. Bambu Lab PAHT-CF, data sheet, p. 1

Odour and emissions

  • Recommended: Process materials in a well ventilated room, or use professional extraction systems. BASF Forward AM Ultrafuse PAHT CF15, data sheet, p. 1
