TPU-CF is a carbon-fibre-filled thermoplastic polyurethane elastomer. A maker sells it for shock absorbers, gaskets and protective components, and makers say the carbon makes the flexible base stiffer and more resistant and cite UV resistance. On the H2C it prints at 230–245 °C with no heated chamber, but makers call for a hardened or ruby nozzle against the abrasive fibre.

Thinly documented Three products, with values from three makers' documents: Spectrum's, Extrudr's and Fiberlogy's. Printing advice

  • Additionally, a nozzle diameter of at least 0.6 mm should be used to prevent clogging and ensure smooth extrusion. Extrudr FLEX HARD CF, data sheet, p. 1
  • The increased stiffness makes the filament easier to print than other flexible TPU materials. Fiberlogy FiberFlex CF, maker's product page or guide, p. 1

Nozzle wear

  • Note: Due to the abrasive nature of the carbon fibers, a hardened nozzle (e.g., hardened steel or ruby nozzle) is strongly recommended. Extrudr FLEX HARD CF, data sheet, p. 1

Uses, limitations and chemicals

What it is sold for

  • FiberFlex CF is ideal for applications requiring a combination of flexibility and strength—such as shock absorbers, gaskets, protective components, industrial structures, or mechanical parts exposed to heavy loads and harsh conditions. Fiberlogy FiberFlex CF, maker's product page or guide
  • Filament S-Flex Carbon is an innovative 3D printing material that seamlessly combines the flexibility of traditional flex filament with the added strength and durability provided by a 20% carbon fiber reinforcement. Spectrum S-Flex Carbon, data sheet, p. 1
  • The carbon reinforces the TPU and makes it more resistant and stiffer. Extrudr FLEX HARD CF, data sheet, p. 1
Finish and accuracy
  • In addition, an excellent carbon look is created. Extrudr FLEX HARD CF, data sheet, p. 1
  • Carbon surface finish Spectrum S-Flex Carbon, data sheet, p. 1
