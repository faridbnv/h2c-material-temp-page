Nylon-GF, maker-undisclosed polyamide is glass-fibre-filled nylon whose makers do not name the polyamide. A maker sells it for industrial tooling and end-use parts, and its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 152 °C. A maker notes that its mechanical properties may change with absorbed moisture. On the H2C a maker's sheet asks for a hardened nozzle and for drying the filament before it prints.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), Nylon-GF, maker-undisclosed polyamide typically reaches a tensile strength of 59.9 MPa at a modulus of 2.82 GPa, and breaks at 7 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 128 MPa at a flexural modulus of 3.7 GPa.

What makers say about printing it

From makers: moisture sensitivity and adhesion between layers

Moisture sensitivity

  • The mechanical properties of Onyx GF may change based on the moisture absorbed from its ambient environment. Markforged Onyx GF, data sheet, p. 2
  • Increased moisture content will result in a more durable part, behavior that is typical of nylon-based materials. Markforged Onyx GF, data sheet, p. 2

Adhesion between layers

  • In tension, most parts are strongest when the print orientation and loading direction are parallel, and weakest when the print orientation and loading direction are perpendicular. Markforged Onyx GF, data sheet, p. 2

Uses, limitations and chemicals

What it is sold for

  • Onyx GF is a chopped-glass-fiber-filled nylon filament built for industrial tooling and end-use parts. Markforged Onyx GF, data sheet, p. 1
  • It delivers the surface finish, dimensional accuracy, and print reliability of Onyx with added functional color and non-conductive properties. Markforged Onyx GF, data sheet, p. 1

What makers warn about

  • The mechanical and thermal properties of Onyx GF may vary with print orientation. Markforged Onyx GF, data sheet, p. 2
Finish and accuracy
  • Available in six colors, Onyx GF brings visual differentiation to the factory floor without added process steps. Markforged Onyx GF, data sheet, p. 1
