PCTG is a glycol-modified polycyclohexylenedimethylene terephthalate. A maker sells it for packaging, electronic housings and biomedical applications, and makers describe low shrinkage and high dimensional stability. Its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 76 °C, below its glass transition. On the H2C it prints at 250–270 °C without chamber heat, and makers advise pre-drying, a maker calling it moderately hygroscopic.

Partly documented Tensile modulus (XY) and price rest on 1 product each, tensile strength (XY) on 2 products and tensile strength (Z) and notched Charpy impact are not published. Each figure is the median of the products that publish it comparably, and beside it the middle half of them; a value on a test bar no document describes as printed, or with no stated test load, is set apart, except a heat deflection of a plastic that cannot crystallise, which its glass transition sets, unless its document points to a moulded or resin bar.

Among the materials

Against PCTG-GF, PCTG's HDT at 0.45 MPa is about the same: 76 °C against 78 °C. Against PLA, PCTG is 9 % stronger flat, and its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 20 °C higher. Among the materials, PCTG has a high elongation at break (XY) and a low tensile modulus (XY) and price: each is in the top or bottom quarter of the materials that publish it. The rest of its key properties sit in the middle half.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), PCTG typically reaches a tensile strength of 49 MPa at a modulus of 1.75 GPa, and breaks at 182 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 69.3 MPa at a flexural modulus of 1.7 GPa.

Thermal properties

PCTG's HDT is typically 76 °C under 0.45 MPa and 64 °C under 1.8 MPa, with its glass transition typically at 84 °C. As published, Vicat softening is typically 87 °C.

Uses, limitations and chemicals

What it is sold for

  • It is suitable for (food) packaging, electronic housings and even biomedical applications. FormFutura AthenaX, data sheet, p. 1
  • The filament contains no bisphenol derivatives (BPA-free) or phthalate-based plasticisers, making it even suitable for food contact. 3DJake PCTG, maker's product page or guide, p. 1

What makers warn about

  • Fiberlogy PCTG is BPA-free , fully recyclable , and, in its Pure TR version, approved for food contact* , which further expands its range of applications. Fiberlogy PCTG, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
  • PCTG offers good resistance to UV, moisture, and heat, making it generally suitable for outdoor use. However, prolonged, permanent outdoor exposure can still cause gradual fading or embrittlement over time. Extrudr PCTG, maker's product page or guide, p.1
Finish and accuracy
  • excellent print surface Spectrum pctg, data sheet, p. 1
  • high dimensional accuracy of the printed elements Spectrum pctg, data sheet, p. 1
  • AthenaX enables you to 3D print parts with high dimensional accuracy. FormFutura AthenaX, data sheet, p. 1

Chemicals and environment
