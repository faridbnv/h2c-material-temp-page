PEEK is polyether ether ketone. Makers sell it for demanding aerospace, automotive, medical and electronic parts. Its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 140–147 °C. It is beyond H2C capability: its products ask for a nozzle at 390–440 °C, past the H2C's limit, and a maker calls it very difficult to print.

Partly documented Notched Charpy impact rests on 1 product, tensile modulus (XY) and HDT at 0.45 MPa on 2 products each and tensile strength (Z) and price are not published. Each figure is the median of the products that publish it comparably, and beside it the middle half of them; a value on a test bar no document describes as printed, or with no stated test load, is set apart, except a heat deflection of a plastic that cannot crystallise, which its glass transition sets, unless its document points to a moulded or resin bar.

Among the materials

Against PC, PEEK is 69 % stiffer and 67 % stronger flat, and its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 21 °C higher. Against PA6/66, PEEK is 64 % stronger flat and 55 % stiffer, and its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 25 °C higher. Among the materials, PEEK has a high tensile strength (XY), elongation at break (XY) and density: each is in the top quarter of the materials that publish it. The rest of its key properties sit in the middle half.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), PEEK typically reaches a tensile strength of 100 MPa at a modulus of 3.63 GPa, and breaks at 28 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 130 MPa at a flexural modulus of 2.93 GPa.

What makers say about printing it

Printing advice

  • Do not let the PEEK material remain in your printer's nozzle for extended periods of time when not extruding. 3D4Makers PEEK, data sheet, p. 2
  • This will lead to speccing and nozzle blockages. 3D4Makers PEEK, data sheet, p. 2
  • print at a low speed. SUNLU PEEK, data sheet, p. 1
  • Your printer should have adequate thermal control in the build chamber throughout the printing process. 3D4Makers PEEK, data sheet, p. 2
  • Lack of process control can also lead to speccing on the part with black specs forming during printing on your part. 3D4Makers PEEK, data sheet, p. 2
  • Lack of process control and inadequate temperatures can also lead to brown discoloration in the part, which is an indication that the parts has not been properly crystalized. 3D4Makers PEEK, data sheet, p. 2

Moisture sensitivity

  • To achieve optimum mechanical properties, it is recommended to pre-dry the material with the above mentioned parameters. Ensinger TECAFIL PEEK EV natural - 1.75 mm, data sheet, p. 1

Nozzle wear

  • For long-term printing, the nozzle needs to be replaced SUNLU PEEK, data sheet, p. 1

Uses, limitations and chemicals

What it is sold for

  • PEEK is used to fabricate items used in demanding applications in aerospace, automotive, oil and gas and medical industries. 3D4Makers PEEK, data sheet, p. 1
  • It is often used in demanding applications such as aerospace, automotive, chemical, and medical industries. iSANMATE PEEK, data sheet, p. 1
  • Aerospace, automotive components, medical devices, electronic components, etc. SUNLU PEEK, data sheet, p. 1

What makers warn about

  • PEEK is a very difficult material to 3D print. 3D4Makers PEEK, data sheet, p. 2

Chemicals and environment

No maker of these products publishes resistance to a named chemical, to water or to sunlight.
