PET-CF is carbon-fibre-filled polyethylene terephthalate. A maker sells it for jigs, fixtures and functional parts, and Bambu Lab for load-bearing structures in hot, humid environments. Its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 124 °C, and a part keeps about 45 % of its flat tensile strength across the layers. On the H2C it needs a hardened nozzle, and makers call for drying and for annealing.

Partly documented Tensile strength (Z) rests on 2 products, notched Charpy impact and price on 3 products each and tensile modulus (XY) and tensile strength (XY) on 4 products each. Each figure is the median of the products that publish it comparably, and beside it the middle half of them; a value on a test bar no document describes as printed, or with no stated test load, is set apart, except a heat deflection of a plastic that cannot crystallise, which its glass transition sets, unless its document points to a moulded or resin bar.

Among the materials

Against PET, PET-CF is 2.3 times as stiff and 20 % stronger flat. Against PET-GF, PET-CF is 62 % more expensive, 24 % stiffer and 8 % weaker across the layers. Among the materials, PET-CF has a high tensile modulus (XY), tensile strength (XY) and density, and a low notched Charpy impact: each is in the top or bottom quarter of the materials that publish it. The rest of its key properties sit in the middle half.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), PET-CF typically reaches a tensile strength of 70 MPa at a modulus of 5.11 GPa, and breaks at 4.25 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 120 MPa at a flexural modulus of 5.32 GPa.

After printing

Four products publish values measured after annealing, on schedules from 80 °C for 12 h to 120 °C for 10 h.

  • We strongly recommend 120°C, 10h annealing to achieve the best performance of the material. Polymaker Fiberon PET-CF17, data sheet, p. 1
  • For a small size model (side length <50 mm), annealing at 80°C for 8 hours is recommended; For a medium-size model (50mm<side length <150 mm), annealing at 80°C for 12 hours is recommended; For a large size model, 100°C for at least 12 hours is recommended. Raise3D Industrial PET CF, data sheet, p. 2
  • After printing, it is recommended to anneal the model in an oven at 80-100°C for 8-12 hours. Raise3D Industrial PET CF, data sheet, p. 2

What makers say about printing it

Supports and removal

  • If PET CF is also used as the support material, please remove the support structure after annealing. Raise3D Industrial PET CF, data sheet, p. 2

Warping and shrinkage

  • After annealing, a maximum of 0.1 % dimensional shrinkage could be observed along the Z-axis, depending on infill and layer height, and no significant dimensional shrinkage along the XY-axes Raise3D Industrial PET CF, data sheet, p. 2
More from makers: printing advice, nozzle wear, moisture sensitivity, odour and emissions and adhesion between layers

Printing advice

  • Before returning the prints, please confirm that the prints have been printed for more than 24h, or placed in an 80-degree oven for 2h to eliminate the internal stress after printing. Polymaker Fiberon PET-CF17, data sheet, p. 1

Nozzle wear

  • Using an abrasion-resistant nozzle, such as hardened steel or a ruby nozzle, is highly recommended. Raise3D Industrial PET CF, data sheet, p. 2

Moisture sensitivity

  • Dry PET CF at 70-80°C for 8-12 hours before printing, as low moisture content is crucial for final printed part quality. Raise3D Industrial PET CF, data sheet, p. 2

Odour and emissions

  • Odor / Almost odorless Raise3D Industrial PET CF, data sheet, p. 1

Adhesion between layers

Uses, limitations and chemicals

What it is sold for

  • strong and cost-effective end-use applications, such as jigs & fixtures and functional parts. Raise3D Industrial PET CF, data sheet, p. 1
  • an ideal choice for engineering-grade composite parts used in many heavy-duty, humidity and high-temp applications. Raise3D Hyper Speed PET CF, data sheet, p. 1
  • Its excellent strength, heat resistance, dimensional stability and low moisture absorption make Bambu PET-CF an ideal choice for load-bearing structures or prints used in high temperature and humid environments. Bambu Lab PET-CF, data sheet, p. 1
Finish and accuracy
  • Bambu PET-CF has excellent high temperature resistance and a soft glow appearance. Bambu Lab PET-CF, data sheet, p. 1
  • It demonstrates a perfect surface finish directly from the printer, thus reducing the need for further treatments. Spectrum PET CF15, data sheet, p. 1
  • Printed part from Hyper Speed PET CF is long-term dimensional accurate and stable with smooth matt surface finishing Raise3D Hyper Speed PET CF, data sheet, p. 1
