PLA blend is a PLA-based bio-blend of polylactic acid with a bio-copolyester. Makers sell it for prototypes, decorative models and some end-use parts, with a matte surface finish, and a maker recommends its own product for lower-temperature applications. On the H2C it prints at 190–230 °C, and most products need no heated chamber.

Thinly documented Nine products, with values from 5 makers' documents. Odour and emissions

  • Yes, it is, as long as the room has good ventilation or an air filtration system for the safety and comfort of the people using the 3D printer. BigRep PRO HT, maker's product page or guide, p. 1

Uses, limitations and chemicals

What it is sold for

  • It is non-toxic and suitable for food contact applications. BigRep PRO HT, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
  • Ideal for both home projects and more demanding educational or design applications. Spectrum GreenyHT, maker's product page or guide, p. 1

What makers warn about

  • PRO HT is recommended for lower temperature applications with its heat deflection temperature (HDT B) of 61°C. BigRep PRO HT, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
  • PRO HT is not UV resistant and is recommended for undoor applications. BigRep PRO HT, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
Finish and accuracy
  • The surface irregularity and matt look effectively mask the layers of printed part. Spectrum GreenyHT, data sheet, p. 1
  • with an attractive matte surface finish. BigRep HI-TEMP, data sheet, p. 1
  • produces rigid, highly accurate parts BigRep HI-TEMP, data sheet, p. 1
