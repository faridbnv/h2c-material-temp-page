CPE-CF is a carbon-fibre-filled CPE copolyester. A maker sells it for parts that need high stiffness and another for applications with long-term load. On the H2C it prints at 250–270 °C without chamber heat, but makers call for a hardened steel or ruby nozzle because the fibres are abrasive.

Among the materials

Among the materials, CPE-CF has a high tensile modulus (XY) and a low elongation at break (XY) and notched Charpy impact: each is in the top or bottom quarter of the materials that publish it. The rest of its key properties sit in the middle half.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), CPE-CF typically reaches a tensile strength of 56 MPa at a modulus of 5.15 GPa, and breaks at 2 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 70 MPa at a flexural modulus of 2.7 GPa.

What makers say about printing it

Printing advice

  • Use of adhesive is necessary to prevent damage of the pad! Fillamentum CPE CF112 Carbon, data sheet, p. 1
  • For best part strength, try to print with the least amount of fan cooling possible. colorFabb XT-CF20, data sheet, p. 2
  • Higher part cooling fan speed is recommended only for supports, bridges and overhangs. Fillamentum CPE CF112 Carbon, data sheet, p. 1
  • For better details and overhangs, increase fan speed. colorFabb XT-CF20, data sheet, p. 2

Nozzle wear

  • It means that it will accelerate the nozzle-wear of brass nozzles faster than unfilled filaments. Fillamentum CPE CF112 Carbon, data sheet, p. 1
  • The hardened steel nozzles are recommended. Fillamentum CPE CF112 Carbon, data sheet, p. 1
  • Ruby or hardened nozzle recommended Spectrum pctg cf10, data sheet, p. 1
  • The CPE CF112 Carbon filament has abrasive properties. Fillamentum CPE CF112 Carbon, data sheet, p. 1

Moisture sensitivity

  • Dry box not necessary Spectrum pctg cf10, data sheet, p. 1
More from makers: warping and shrinkage and adhesion between layers

Warping and shrinkage

  • The use of carbon fibres increases the thermal resistance of the filament, further reduces shrinkage and gives the characteristic “carbon” matt appearance to the printed models. Spectrum pctg cf10, data sheet, p. 1

Adhesion between layers

  • The contained fibres ensure functionality, great printability, dimensional stability and layer adhesion. Fillamentum CPE CF112 Carbon, data sheet, p. 1
