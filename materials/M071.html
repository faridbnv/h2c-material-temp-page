PPA-GF is glass-fibre-filled polyphthalamide. Makers sell it for end-use automotive, aerospace and electronics parts. Semicrystalline, it carries light load well above its glass transition, and its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 196 °C. On the H2C every product needs a hardened nozzle, makers advise drying at 80–140 °C, 4–12 h, and some ask for more chamber heat than the H2C gives.

Among the materials

Against PPA-CF, PPA-GF is 14 % less stiff and 7 % weaker flat, and its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 24 °C lower. Against PA6-CF, PPA-GF is 50 % stiffer, 43 % stronger flat and 20 % stronger across the layers. Among the materials, PPA-GF has a high tensile modulus (XY), tensile strength (XY), tensile strength (Z) and HDT at 0.45 MPa, and a low elongation at break (XY): each is in the top or bottom quarter of the materials that publish it. Its density and price sit in the middle half.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), PPA-GF typically reaches a tensile strength of 100 MPa at a modulus of 6.56 GPa, and breaks at 2.7 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 159 MPa at a flexural modulus of 4.71 GPa.

Thermal properties

Its products put HDT at 84⁠–⁠220 °C under 0.45 MPa and 80⁠–⁠118 °C under 1.8 MPa, against a glass transition of 80 °C. Semicrystalline, it carries light load well above its glass transition. As published, Vicat softening is typically 233 °C and melting typically 244 °C.

After printing

Three products publish values measured after annealing, at 100 °C for 8 h.

  • After the printing, it is recommended to anneal the model in the oven at 80-100°C for 4 to 8 hours and cool the room temperature naturally. Raise3D Industrial PPA GF, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
  • After printing, it is recommended to anneal the printed part at 80-100°C for 8 hours. Raise3D Hyper Core PPA GF25, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
  • Annealing can significantly increase the heat resistance (HDT), stiffness, and strength of your PPA-GF parts. Siraya Tech Fibreheart PPA-GF, data sheet, p. 5

What makers say about printing it

Nozzle wear

  • Using abrasion resistance nozzle, as hardened steel and above grade nozzle is highly recommended. Raise3D Industrial PPA GF, data sheet, p. 2

Printing advice

  • An all-metal hotend capable of reaching and maintaining 320°C is required. Siraya Tech Fibreheart PPA-GF, data sheet, p. 4
