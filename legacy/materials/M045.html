PEBA is a polyether block amide, a thermoplastic elastomer. Makers sell it for protective gear, phone cases, shoe outsoles and sports equipment, and a maker cites its high energy return. Printed flat it reaches a tensile strength of 30 MPa and breaks at 1,050 % elongation. On the H2C it prints without chamber heat, and makers advise drying it, a maker thoroughly before every print.

Partly documented Tensile modulus (XY), tensile strength (Z) and price rest on 1 product each and notched Charpy impact is not published. Among the materials

Against TPU 95A class, PEBA's tensile strength (XY) is about the same: 30 MPa against 29 MPa. Against TPU 85A class and softer, PEBA is 36 % stronger flat. Among the materials, PEBA has a high elongation at break (XY) and a low tensile modulus (XY), tensile strength (XY), tensile strength (Z) and density: each is in the top or bottom quarter of the materials that publish it. Its price sits in the middle half.

What makers say about printing it

Moisture sensitivity

  • Dry the filament thoroughly before every print, even if the spool is new. Siraya Tech Rebound PEBA 95A, data sheet, p. 1

Printing advice

  • Print the first layer slower (e.g., 15-20mm/s) and slightly hotter (e.g., +5°C on nozzle) for best adhesion. Siraya Tech Rebound PEBA 95A, data sheet, p. 1
More from makers: nozzle wear, warping and shrinkage, adhesion between layers and supports and removal

Nozzle wear

  • Ruby or hardened nozzle recommended: No Spectrum peba, data sheet, p. 1

Warping and shrinkage

  • 100°C for full plate large model print to prevent warping Siraya Tech Rebound PEBA 95A, data sheet, p. 1

Adhesion between layers

  • Excellent interlayer adhesion Spectrum peba, data sheet, p. 1

Supports and removal

  • Supports printed in PEBA can be challenging due to strong layer bonding. Siraya Tech Rebound PEBA 95A, data sheet, p. 1

Uses, limitations and chemicals

What it is sold for

  • High-Performance Sporting Goods : Running shoe midsoles, custom functional insoles, responsive bicycle saddles. Siraya Tech Rebound PEBA 95A, data sheet, p. 1
  • As a high-performance thermoplastic elastomer filament, it can be widely applied in high-end shoe materials, sports equipment, medical devices, industrial components, cushioning packaging, and more. eSUN PEBA90A, data sheet, p. 1
  • The application for shoe outsoles is possible thanks to high energy return, abrasion resistance and low density. Fillamentum Flexfill PEBA 90A, data sheet, p. 1

What makers warn about

  • Active foaming materials naturally expand in the hotend, causing oozing during travel moves. Siraya Tech Rebound PEBA Air 70A–95A, data sheet, p. 1
  • Standard retraction settings are often ineffective. Siraya Tech Rebound PEBA Air 70A–95A, data sheet, p. 1
Finish and accuracy
  • Briefly applying heat (e.g., heat gun on low setting, very quick pass with a torch flame) can sometimes smooth surfaces or reduce fuzziness, but exercise extreme caution to avoid melting or burning the part. Siraya Tech Rebound PEBA 95A, data sheet, p. 1
  • The material has great optical properties, gloss, and transparency. Fillamentum Flexfill PEBA 90A, data sheet, p. 1
