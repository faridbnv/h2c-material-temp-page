TPU 85A class and softer is a soft thermoplastic polyurethane elastomer with a glass transition of -46 °C. Makers sell it for insoles, prosthetic components, wearables and cushioning parts. A part keeps about 57 % of its flat tensile strength across the layers. On the H2C the sheets that say allow a standard nozzle, but a maker says its softness makes printing challenging and dry filament crucial.

Partly documented Notched Charpy impact rests on 1 product, tensile strength (Z) on 2 products, price on 3 products and tensile modulus (XY) on 4 products. Each figure is the median of the products that publish it comparably, and beside it the middle half of them; a value on a test bar no document describes as printed, or with no stated test load, is set apart, except a heat deflection of a plastic that cannot crystallise, which its glass transition sets, unless its document points to a moulded or resin bar.

Among the materials

Against TPU-CF, TPU 85A class and softer is 69 % less expensive. Against TPU 95A class, TPU 85A class and softer is 59 % weaker across the layers, 52 % less stiff and 30 % less expensive. Among the materials, TPU 85A class and softer has a high elongation at break (XY) and notched Charpy impact, and a low tensile modulus (XY), tensile strength (XY) and tensile strength (Z): each is in the top or bottom quarter of the materials that publish it. Its density and price sit in the middle half.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), TPU 85A class and softer typically reaches a tensile strength of 22 MPa at a modulus of 12.7 MPa, and breaks at 680 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 1.55 MPa at a flexural modulus of 23 MPa.

Uses, limitations and chemicals

What it is sold for

  • Medical & Orthopedic: Custom insoles and prosthetic components Recreus 70A, data sheet, p. 1
  • this filament offers unparalleled softness and flexibility, ideal for wearables, cushioning components, and ergonomic designs. colorFabb TPU85A, data sheet, p. 1
  • Due to its low evaporation rate, FLEX Semisoft is particularly suitable for automotive interior applications. Extrudr FLEX SEMISOFT, data sheet, p. 1

What makers warn about

  • While TPU 85A presents printing challenges due to its softer nature, careful adjustment of print settings and ensuring dry filament conditions are crucial for success. colorFabb TPU85A, data sheet, p. 1
  • The nozzle may have impurities after printing for a long time. eSUN eFlex, data sheet, p. 2
